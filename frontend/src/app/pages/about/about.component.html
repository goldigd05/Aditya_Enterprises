Who We Are

Your Trusted Hardware Partner

Aditya Enterprises has been a reliable name in architectural hardware for over a decade. We specialize in supplying premium door handles, hinges, locks, closers, glass fittings and cabinet hardware to builders, contractors, architects and homeowners across India.

Our commitment to quality, competitive pricing and dependable service has helped us build long-lasting relationships with hundreds of satisfied clients.
